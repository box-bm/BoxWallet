BoxWallet is a simple, private personal finance app to keep your money organized.

<b>Everything at a glance</b>
See your balance, latest transactions and active loans on a single dashboard.

<b>Income & expenses</b>
• Record income and expenses in seconds
• Organize them by category
• Choose your currency

<b>Loans & installments</b>
• Track loans installment by installment
• See paid vs. pending installments with progress
• Get reminders so you never miss a payment

<b>Analytics</b>
• Balance trend charts
• Spending breakdown by category

<b>Reports & backup</b>
• Export your reports
• Back up and restore your data with Google Drive

<b>Your style</b>
Material You design with light/dark themes and dynamic colors.
